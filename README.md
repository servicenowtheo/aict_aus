# Take Control of AI with the ServiceNow AI Control Tower - Intake to Governance to Deployment

#### Introduction

In today’s rapidly evolving technological landscape, Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a cornerstone for organizations aiming to accelerate operations, boost revenue, and enhance efficiency. Teams across product development, business, IT operations, and more are eager to harness AI’s potential. However, with great power comes great responsibility. Risk and compliance teams, alongside legal and governance leaders, must ensure AI is implemented ethically and responsibly, adhering to regulations and internal policies. &#x20;

The ServiceNow AI Control Tower Solution addresses this need by providing a unified platform to manage AI across the organization – whether developed in-house or sourced from third parties. This solution empowers organizations to speed up AI adoption and address/manage: &#x20;

* AI Inventory: Maintain a comprehensive view of AI systems, models, and datasets.&#x20;
* Lifecycle Governance: From intake to deployment and monitoring, ensure ethical and compliant AI use.&#x20;
* Risk Mitigation: Identify and address AI-related risks through assessments and controls.&#x20;
* Leadership Insights: Support emerging roles like the Chief AI Officer (CAIO) and AI Center of Excellence (AI CoE) with dashboards and insights.&#x20;
* AI Observation: Continuously monitor and evaluate AI agents' performance through metrics and log traces.
* AI Agent Discovery: Automatically discover AI across the enterprise—hyperscalers, SaaS platforms, dev platforms.
* Secure AI: Track AI access, security posture metrics, and guardrails—taking action through automated workflows while maintaining control across the entire AI landscape.
* Measure AI: Quantify AI's business impact with comprehensive metrics tracking adoption rates, realized value, and ROI.

Key personas, such as CAIOs, CIOs, CTOs, Chief Risk Officers (CROs), and Chief Compliance Officers, face challenges in aligning AI strategies with compliance requirements, managing investments, and ensuring ethical development. This lab guide walks you through the AI Control Tower workflow: Intake, Assess, Build, Review, and Deploy & Monitor, demonstrating how ServiceNow tackles these challenges head-on!&#x20;
