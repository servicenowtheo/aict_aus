# Lab 4: Build and Test – Implement, Analyze, & Attest Controls

#### Lab 4 Objectives

In the **Build and test** state, business users implement the controls associated with their AI system management processes. **During this state, Controls are attested to and issues are identified and managed.**

**Scenario**

**Josh Warner**, the AI Risk and Compliance Analyst, will review the risks and controls associated to the Cloud Dimensions SkyTrack AI system and assign **Control Attestations** to **Mary** to verify the implementation of the associated controls.<br>

Any issues identified during this phase will be managed through **actionable remediation tasks**.\
As part of this process, once attestations are sent out Mary will be responsible for ensuring the controls are implemented, attesting to their effectiveness, and addressing any related issues.
