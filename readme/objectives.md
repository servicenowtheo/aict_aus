# Objectives

This workshop introduces the ServiceNow AI Control Tower Solution, focusing on several AI asset functions and intermediate concepts of AI risk management. The lab will start with foundational elements like AI systems' metrics, AI strategy, Value & Adoption, Risk & Compliance, and Security & Privacy.  As the lab continues, you will progress through an AI asset lifecycle to process, review, & govern AI systems responsibly. Key highlights include:

* Understanding AI Status, AI Assets, AI Value, & Other AI-Related Metrics.
* Registering AI Systems in the AI Inventory.
* Conducting impact and risk assessments to ensure compliance.
* Implementing controls and managing issues during development.
* Reviewing AI systems pre-deployment and monitoring them post-deployment.

We will explore this lab through three personas in our sample company, Cloud Dimensions:

1\. Mary Cruse – a product owner of an AI System on the Cybersecurity Operations team. Mary will submit a request to register the AI System and will also action on related tasks, such as a formal Impact Assessment, control attestations, and issue remediations.

2\. Alene Rabeck – the AI Steward and a key member of the AI Center of Excellence (CoE). Alene monitors all AI Systems in use across the organization and has oversight on the system onboarding process.

3\. Josh Warner – an AI Risk and Compliance Analyst who performs risk assessments and ensures necessary controls are implemented on deployed systems.
