# Lab 3: Assess – Evaluate the AI Use Case Impacts

The **Assessment phase** focuses on completing the **Impact Assessment** for the AI system use case. This AI impact assessment ensures that your AI system is both effective and respectful of individuals' rights. By identifying and addressing potential impacts on privacy, non-discrimination, and other human rights, you can help ensure that your AI system complies with global regulations and frameworks, such as the EU AI Act and the NIST AI Risk and Compliance Framework. This will also help ensure that your system operates ethically and responsibly in accordance with organizational AI policies.

Based on the responses provided, relevant **risks and controls** will be identified. These will then be reviewed by **Josh Warner**, the AI Risk and Compliance Analyst, before being finalized and formally associated with the AI system ensuring alignment with both ethical principles and regulatory requirements.

**Scenario**

An AI System Impact Assessment has been automatically generated to identify potential risks and controls for this new system. The assessment has been routed to Mary Cruse, who was identified in the initial intake process as the AI Product Owner. Mary will navigate to the AI Control Tower to complete this and all following tasks relating to their AI system.

**Privacy and Data Protection**

1. Does your AI system collect or process personal data (e.g. health data, biometric, racial/ethical data)? **Yes**

5. Can individuals request access to, correct, or delete their personal data from your AI system? **Yes**

**Non-Discrimination and Fairness**

1. Could your AI system potentially discriminate against individuals based on protected characteristics such as age, gender, race, disability, or socio-economic status? **No**
2. Have you tested the AI system for bias or fairness, particularly in sensitive areas like recruitment, lending, or healthcare? **Yes**
3. Does your AI system make significant decisions that could impact people’s rights or opportunities? **Yes**
4. Have you taken steps to ensure that the AI system makes decisions that are fair, transparent, and do not lead to unjust outcomes? **Yes**
5. Does your AI system allow for human intervention in cases where a decision seems unfair or biased? **No**

**Transparency and Accountability**

1. Can the decisions made by your AI system be explained to users in a way that is understandable to a non-expert? **No**
2. Does your AI system provide users with clear information about how decisions are made? **Partially**
3. Is there a mechanism in place for individuals to challenge or appeal decisions made by your AI system? **No**

**Freedom of Expression and Information**

1. Does your AI system allow for the free flow of information and prevent undue censorship (e.g., in media, social networks, or content moderation)? **No**
2. Could your AI system restrict access to certain information or censor certain viewpoints, either intentionally or unintentionally? **Yes**

**Right to Remain Anonymous and Consent**

1. Does your AI system allow individuals to use its services without revealing personally identifiable information, where possible? **No**
2. Can users exercise their right to remain anonymous, or is their personal data always required to use the system? **No, personal data is required**

**Human Oversight and Control**

1. Does the AI system provide a way for human operators to intervene and override decisions made by the AI, especially in cases that affect fundamental rights? **No**
2. Are there clear guidelines for human operators to follow in case they need to intervene in AI decision-making? **No**

**Impact Monitoring and Mitigation**

1. Have you set up a process to monitor the AI system’s impact on fundamental rights after deployment (e.g., monitoring for discrimination or privacy violations)? **Yes**
2. Do you have measures in place to address any negative impacts on fundamental rights if identified (e.g., correcting biased outputs or ensuring privacy safeguards are upheld)? **Yes**
